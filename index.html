A new kind of gathering place

Where Orchard
Road opens up.

67 on Scotts is the newest experiential event space on Orchard Road by 67 Pall Mall. A place to make an evening of dinner, drop in for a glass, or bring people together around a shared idea.

Its atmosphere shifts with the occasion: intimate dining one moment, a gallery-style showcase or private gathering the next.
